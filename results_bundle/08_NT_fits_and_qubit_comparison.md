# 08: N_D and N_T fit lines, and the comparison with qubits (2026-10-02)

Every number here is regenerated by `compute_nt_comparison.py`.

## Two different counts

| Count | What it charges | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **N_D** (per-phase, convention A) | 1 per non-trivial ζ₉ phase (so a T-type step counts **2**), 1 per level-4 step, 1 per R | Comparison with the literature's R-count for C+R. It is not a fault-tolerant cost |
| **N_T** (T-cost) | Qutrit T gates (T = diag(1,ζ₉,ζ₉⁸)) actually emitted: T-type = 1, level-4 = R = 7 T (unitary gadgets) or 4 T (measurement gadgets) | **Fault-tolerant cost:** the number of T magic states to distill and inject. It equals the T-count of the emitted Clifford+T circuit (`compiler/cd_to_ct.py`) |

## N_D fit (900 matrices, convention A, residual-R fix)
**N_D = 3.94 + 5.16·log₃(1/ε) = 3.94 + 10.82·log₁₀(1/ε)**, R² = 0.912, giving **~112 at ε = 10⁻¹⁰**.
- **Symmetry-copy selection barely moves it:** 1.04 + 5.20·log₃ for the min-N_D copy (n = 210).
- **The cheapest-T copy also has essentially the lowest N_D** (1.06 + 5.20·log₃).
- **Convention B** (R free) would give 2.94 + 4.82·log₃. Convention A was adopted.
- **For comparison:** C+R N_R is 5.14 (Householder) and 4.11 (Exhaustive) per log₃. The gate-set-independent SU(3) covering bound is 4.90·log₃.

  - measurement: 10.9 + 6.44·log₃;
  - merged: 14.1 + 7.23·log₃;
  - unitary: 18.7 + 9.73·log₃.
  - The 900-matrix as-given unitary fit is 16.6 + 10.0·log₃.
- **Recommended single line:** measurement model, **N_T ≈ 1.8 + 6.4·log₃(1/ε)**. Measurement and feed-forward are standard on fault-tolerant hardware. Give unitary + merging, 3.3 + 7.2·log₃, as the no-measurement alternative.

- **The slopes may still fall** with best-of-K over genuinely different approximants. That awaits Nick's larger `--top_n` rerun; at f = 4 it gave another ~37%.

## Comparison with qubits

**(1) Rotation for rotation.** One qutrit R_z vs one qubit R_z:

| | N_T per rotation | per log₃(1/ε) | at 10⁻¹⁰ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qutrit C+D, measurement | 1.8 + 6.42·log₃ | 6.42 | 136 |
| Qutrit C+D, unitary + merging | 3.3 + 7.21·log₃ | 7.21 | 154 |
| Qubit Clifford+T, deterministic (Ross–Selinger 1403.2975) | ≈ 3·log₂(1/ε) | 4.75 | 100 |
| Qubit Clifford+T, RUS (Bocharov–Roetteler–Svore, PRL 114, 080502) | 9.2 + 1.15·log₂(1/ε) | 1.82 | 47 |

A qutrit rotation costs ~1.4–1.5× a deterministic qubit rotation, and ~3× an RUS one. But a qutrit rotation acts on a 3-dimensional space, so this comparison is not equal footing.

**(2) Same computational space** (Gustafson et al. §5 convention): an arbitrary single-qutrit gate is 6 diagonal rotations; its two-qubit emulation needs ≥ 10 single-qubit rotations.

| At ε = 10⁻¹⁰ | T per arbitrary qutrit gate | vs 2-qubit deterministic (997 T) | vs 2-qubit RUS (474 T) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C+D, measurement** | 818 | **0.82×** | 1.73× |
| **C+D, unitary + merging** | 926 | **0.93×** | 1.95× |
| C+D, unitary | 1,236 | 1.24× | 2.61× |
| C+R Householder, measurement | 2,662 | 2.67× | 5.61× |
| C+R Householder, unitary | 4,658 | 4.67× | 9.83× |

**Recommended framing:**
- **Against deterministic synthesis:** qutrit C+D reaches parity with, or slightly beats, two-qubit Clifford+T emulation (0.8–0.9× in T-count).
- **Against qubit RUS:** C+D is ~1.7–2× behind.
- **Against qutrit C+R:** C+D is 3–4× cheaper.

## Caveats (state them in the paper)
1. **Not the same resource.** A qutrit T-state from QRM₃(2) 8→1 costs 0.3–4× a qubit T-state from 15→1 in raw states, depending on target and error rate. Modern qubit factories (cultivation, Litinski) have no qutrit analogue yet.
2. **Physical footprint is not modelled:** one qutrit vs two qubits, and the corresponding code distances.
3. **Model mismatch in the baselines.** The deterministic qubit baseline uses no measurement tricks. The cleanest like-for-like pairing is unitary + merging (0.93×) vs Ross–Selinger.
4. **Sample size.** The C+D fits use 210 of Nick's matrices (30 θ × 7 f). The as-given unitary fit agrees with the 900-matrix fit (9.73 vs 10.0 per log₃).
